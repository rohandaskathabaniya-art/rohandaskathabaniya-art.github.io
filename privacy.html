Privacy

Short and plain. Last updated 20 September 2026.

What this site collects

Nothing about you on a server. This website has no accounts, no database, no analytics and no advertising cookies at the moment. If that changes, this page will be updated first.

Free tools

All calculations happen in your browser. The text and numbers you type are never sent to me or anyone else. The job tracker saves its list in your browser's local storage on your own device only.

What is stored in your browser

Your light or dark mode choice, and the job tracker list if you use it. You can remove both by clearing your site data.

Contact form

The form does not send anything to a server. It opens your email app. What you send then is an ordinary email to contact@rohandaskathabaniya.com.np, and I use it only to reply to you.

Third parties

Pages load fonts from Google Fonts, which means Google can see your IP address when a page loads. The site is hosted on GitHub Pages, which keeps its own server logs. Links to LinkedIn, GitHub and other sites are governed by their own policies.

Questions

Email contact@rohandaskathabaniya.com.np.
